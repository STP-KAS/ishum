Invoice statuses: New, Processing, Settled, Expired, Invalid. Webhook body is the invoice JSON. Header X-Ishum-Event is InvoiceSettled etc.

Every invoice carries sequence: kaspa-l1, feeAsset: KAS, feeSompi. Settlement methods carry kind (native / reserved-native / guest-iou) and freeze on USDT. Quote currency is not the fee asset.

Checkout poll does not need the token. Marking KAS settled without a tx requires ISHUM_DEMO=1.
