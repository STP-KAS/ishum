Slogan is the sequencer, not the money: sequenced on Kaspa. You do not need Tether to sequence on. You need a unit that does not jump 8% while the tx confirms. Those can be different objects.

What “sequenced” means here

Sutton, 11 Sep 2026: global DeFi is not sequential. The world updates in parallel. Relative order matters inside a related sub-series (a double-spend is the local case). It does not follow that every mutation must share a constant number of sequential bottlenecks. Apps should be pushed into partitioned state, not the base into shared state. If DeFi truly had to go through those bottlenecks, zk would not save it.

A till already is that partition. This invoice’s pay and the next shop’s pay do not share a mutex. L1 orders this payment against double-spend. It does not run the world’s books through one AMM. A global USDT/kUSD pool everyone spends would be the bottleneck he is warning about. Ishum does not have one. Quote lives on the merchant’s machine. Settlement is a UTXO (or a labeled guest IOU) to that merchant’s address.

USDT in does not delete Kaspa decentralization. It adds a centralized money on top. If that’s the product, the till says so. If USDT were gas, miners would be economically upstream of a freeze-capable issuer. That rail is refused: fee asset is KAS, full stop.

What BTCPay already got right

Self-hosted invoice engine. Watch-only. POS keypad + cart. Pay button. Webhook. Greenfield-shaped API. Lightning existed because Bitcoin L1 is too slow for a counter. Kaspa L1 is ~1s; default settle is 10 acceptances ≈ 10 seconds. A Kaspa BTCPay plugin is the documented altcoin path and is more work than this process. Mapping: Altcoins → plugin-btcpay/.
