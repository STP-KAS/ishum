# Ishum — explainer

Basis: BTCPay “What is BTCPay Server?” (XHj7EVUunJ4, 1:33).
Same beats. Kaspa facts instead of Bitcoin/Lightning. No slogans beyond the name.

## Voice (facts)

A counter, an online store, or an API that needs to take payment.

A processor prices the invoice, watches the chain, and tells you when to hand over the goods.

Most processors also hold the money, take a cut, and can freeze the account.

Ishum is that processor, running on your machine.

The customer gets an invoice. They pay in Kaspa native, a Kaspa stable if one exists, or a Tether-class alternative on Kaspa.

The payment is a Kaspa transaction. Confirmations arrive in seconds. Bitcoin needed Lightning for that speed. Kaspa does it on L1.

Funds go to the address you set. Ishum never has the keys.

Point of sale is a keypad or a cart. Online is a pay button and a webhook.

Ishum. Sequenced on Kaspa.
